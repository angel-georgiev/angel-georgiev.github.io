# Landing Page Research — Active Personal-Brand Heroes (11 Nov 2026)

Sources: about 24 expert homepages, opened as text only, so the visuals were inferred from alt text and filenames. Plus speaker-site roundups (nanoglobals.com, speakerflow.com).

## Patterns that read as ACTIVE
- **Verb-led or imperative headline:** Julie Dirksen "Create World-class Learning", Cathy Moore "Design training that matters.", Tamsen Webster "BUILD MESSAGES THAT BUILD BUY-IN."
- **Rotating verb in the headline:** zackkass.com ("live well" → lead / teach / parent / build…).
- **Action CTA:** Jay Baer's "See Jay in Action" button opens a video carousel.
- **Video loop or media clip first:** marieforleo.com, vinhgiang.com, juliantreasure.com.
- **"Now" freshness:** a ticker with dates (Forleo); a dated post on top (Seth Godin); a /now page (sive.rs).
- **Counter strip directly under the hero:** Vinh Giang (16M+ / 250K+ / 45+), Julian Treasure (TED views, students).
- **Award as eyebrow:** Jay Baer "Voted #1 Global Guru".
- **Doors routed by visitor intent, phrased as verbs:** Cathy Moore "What do you want to do?".

## Patterns that read as PASSIVE
- Job title as headline: adamgrant.net.
- "Hi, I'm X": jamesclear.com, garyvaynerchuk.com.
- Name only: sive.rs, sahilbloom.com.
- A static portrait on its own: scotthyoung.com, usablelearning.com.

## Applied in landing v2
- Award eyebrow: "Обучител на годината · Европа 2026".
- Short H1 with a rotating verb: "Уча учители и екипи да [преподават / планират / оценяват / учат / работят] с AI." Static under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Action photo (DSC_4006, laughing while writing) with a live "Сега" card and a "20 000+" badge.
- Verb CTAs routed by intent: "Запази сесия за подкрепа — 45 мин · 29 €" (primary) and "Обучение за училище — 1 кредит" (secondary). The CV becomes a text link plus a nav item.
- "Този месец" strip: Преподавам / Помагам / Пиша. Updated monthly from data.
- Static number strip. Animated counters were rejected on purpose (earlier research: they read as gimmicky).
- "Как да работим заедно": 3 offers (session / school training / talks & media).
- "Вижте ме в действие": TEDx, Bloomberg TV, БНТ, Forbes cards, plus a line of other outlets.
- Book block.
- Motion budget: one moving element (the rotating verb). The "Сега" dot pulse is subtle and off under reduced motion.
